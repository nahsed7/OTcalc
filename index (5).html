Calculation rules

08:00–17:00 = normal working time. The lunch break is deducted every day it overlaps the shift, so 08:00–17:00 on a normal day = 8 normal hours, not 9.
Before 08:00 and after 17:00 = ordinary OT.
Sunday and listed holidays = all OT (lunch deducted too).
Half day: a normal day with 3+ hours worked and check-out at or before the half-day time below. It is not counted as a day and gets a fixed normal-OT credit instead of normal hours.
Days worked counts only normal days: no Sundays, no holidays, no half days.
Overnight and multi-day shifts are supported: a check-out on a later date (In 9/14 07:57, Out 9/15 07:59) is stored as 07:59+1 and counted as one continuous shift, with normal time only 08:00–17:00 and everything else OT. You can type +1 yourself in the End box. OT rounds down to completed 30-minute units.
Importing a new sheet or pasted text clears the earlier table first (nothing is saved until Calculate & Save).
